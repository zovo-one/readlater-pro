Search inside everything you saved

Free search matches titles, URLs and tags. That works until the list gets long, and then you are hunting for an article whose title you cannot remember. Full-text search reads the words inside every article you saved.

What Pro adds

Full-text search with no daily cap

Free gives you twenty full-text searches a day, which is plenty for a small list. Pro removes the counter, so a library of a few thousand articles stays searchable on the days you are actually digging.

Pro also makes Listen unlimited (free is 3 articles a day) and includes Markdown export (free gets one try). Saving, reader view, the side panel, tags, highlights, imports, reading progress, offline reading, the backup, CSV and bookmarks exports and your reading stats are free and uncapped. Nothing here gets taken away later.

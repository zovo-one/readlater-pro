ReadLater Pro by Zovo v1.3.0

ReadLater Pro is up to date. You've saved 0 articles and finished 0. Here's what changed in this release.

What's new in 1.3.0

Changes in this release

New

Highlights and notes

Select any text in reader view to highlight it in one of four colors, and add a note if you like. Your highlights come back every time you open the article, they're all in one place in the side panel. In reader view you can copy or download an article's highlights as Markdown; exporting every highlight at once is part of Pro, with one free try.

New

Listen to an article

Press Listen in reader view and ReadLater reads the article aloud with a voice installed on your computer, so it works offline too. Pick the speed and the voice, skip a paragraph forward or back, and follow along as the current paragraph is marked. Chrome's online voices are in the list as well, marked online. Free for 3 articles a day, and unlimited with Pro.

New

Read right in the side panel

Articles can now open inside the side panel, so you can read next to the page you're on. Choose "This panel" in the side panel's view options, or right-click an article and pick Read in panel.

New

Bring your library from Pocket, Omnivore and others

The import page now reads Pocket's final export (the ZIP brings tags, archive status and highlights; the CSV alone brings tags and archive status), Omnivore's export with the full article text and highlights, Instapaper and Raindrop CSV files, any browser's bookmarks file and a plain list of links. You see what's in the file before anything is added, and a real progress bar while it runs.

New

Take your data anywhere

Export your library as Markdown with your highlights, as a CSV in Pocket's format, as a bookmarks file any browser can import, or as a full backup that brings back your tags and highlights when you restore it. The CSV, bookmarks and backup exports are free; Markdown export is part of Pro, and everyone gets one free try.

New

A side panel that does more

Save the page you're on or every tab in a window from the panel (with the full text when ReadLater can read the page, otherwise as a link), filter to quick reads or long reads, and let Rediscover pick something you saved a while ago. The panel now remembers your tab and sort order, shows how long your list would take to read, and opens your reading stats.

Improved

Reader view that fits the way you read

Choose a serif, sans, extra-readable or monospace font and set the line spacing. The new Auto theme follows your computer's dark mode. The font and spacing you picked in Settings used to be ignored; they now apply. At the end of an article, Next unread takes you straight to the next one.

Fixed in this release

  • The side panel updates while it's open, so an article you just finished leaves Unread right away.
  • Picking a tag keeps your tab, search and sort instead of showing everything.
  • Deleting from the list can be undone, and Ctrl or Cmd shortcuts no longer trigger list actions.
  • The unread count on the toolbar icon shows on normal web pages again.
  • Big imports now save in batches and check what was actually saved, so a slow import no longer shows as a failure while it's still running.
  • Pages that reader view couldn't fully parse now show their saved text instead of "No content available".
  • Data exports no longer include your license key.

Everything above is free except unlimited Listen and Markdown export, which come with Pro. Your saved articles, tags and settings carry over exactly as you left them.

Questions people ask

Where are my saved articles stored?

In this browser, on your computer. ReadLater keeps each article's text so you can read it offline, and nothing about your library is uploaded. That also means a cleared browser profile clears your list, so export a backup from Settings once in a while.

Does ReadLater Pro send anything to a server?

Your articles, searches and reading stats stay in your browser. Pro confirms your license key with Zovo's server from time to time, sending only the key and the extension's name. If you uninstall, Chrome opens a zovo.one page whose address carries the version, how long it was installed, its update count and the extension's ID. Pictures load from the sites that published them.

What happens if I stop paying for Pro?

If you cancel, Pro stays on until the license server reports the plan has ended. After that you're on the free version again, with your whole library where you left it, full-text search goes back to 20 a day, Listen to 3 articles a day, and Markdown export switches off. The backup, CSV and bookmarks exports stay free.

Can I get a refund?

Monthly plans can be cancelled at any time. Lifetime is a one-time payment delivered instantly, so it's final once the key and archive have been sent. If anything is faulty or not as described, it gets fixed or refunded.

Open the license page, choose Enter License Key, and paste the key that starts with ZOVO-. You'll find it in your purchase email and on your zovo.one dashboard. Can't find it? Email support@zovo.one.

Can I stop this page from opening after updates?

Yes. Tick the box at the bottom of this page, or switch off "Show what's new after an update" in Settings under Notifications. Updates will still install, just without this tab.
